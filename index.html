Our story

Sani's Bakes began in a home kitchen in Doha

What started as cakes for family and friends grew into a bakery trusted by families across Qatar. Today, Sani's Bakes is the brand of SANIS CONFECTIONERY TRADING WLL, a registered Qatari company.

We bake every cake to order, so it reaches you fresh. Each design is planned with you, from the theme and colours to the name piped on top, so your cake looks like your celebration and nobody else's.

Birthdays, baby showers, graduations, anniversaries and farewells: if it's a moment worth remembering, we would love to bake for it.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

SANIS CONFECTIONERY TRADING WLLCR No. 255518Last updated: 29 October 2026

Because every cake is a fresh, perishable food made to your order, our refund rules follow the nature of the product while respecting your rights under Qatar's Consumer Protection Law No. 8 of 2008.

1. Cancellations

  • More than 72 hours before the due date: full refund of the advance.
  • 48–72 hours before: 50% of the advance is refunded.
  • Less than 48 hours before: the advance is not refundable, as ingredients and custom toppers have already been prepared.
  • Instead of a refund, you may move your order to a new date within 30 days, subject to availability.

2. Returns

Fresh food cannot be returned once it has been delivered or collected. Please check your cake at hand-over.

3. Problems with your cake

  • If your cake is damaged on our delivery, materially different from the confirmed order, or has a quality problem, contact us within 6 hours of receiving it on WhatsApp +974 7013 9002 with photos, and keep the cake.
  • After review we will offer a replacement, a partial refund or a full refund, depending on the issue.
  • Small handmade variations in colour, shape or decoration are not defects.

4. When we cancel

If we must cancel an order for any reason, you receive a full refund of all amounts paid.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method or by bank transfer within 7–14 working days.
